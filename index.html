Enduro Phase I Fees*

Phase I of Enduro 2026 will primarily be online. Hence there won't be any tuition fees.

Enduro Phase II Fees**

Phase II of Enduro 2027 will be a residential program conducted in the vicinity of one of the partnering educational institutions. Accommodation and Food will be arranged by the organizers along with tuition and other infrastructure. Participants will be charged with nominal and subsidized fees.

Note: Wisdom Eye shall offer stipends to all the participants on merit basis

Above scholarships shall be waived off from the final fees of Phase II
