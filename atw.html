ENDURO TRACK 1

Annual Technical Workshops (ATW)

“To boost our competence”

An attempt to bring the best of both worlds to students: bridging cutting-edge industry software craftsmanship with world-class academic rigour.

💼
Pillar 1

Industry Experience

  • Get to know how things work in corporations: Understand enterprise architecture, agile sprints, and production deployment cycles.
  • Work with skilled professionals: Gain direct mentorship from seasoned industry tech leads and distinguished engineers.
  • Acquire the experience of building reliable software: Build robust, production-grade systems ready for real-world stress loads.
🎓
Pillar 2

Academic Experience

  • Learn concepts right from the fundamentals: Develop crystal-clear conceptual clarity guided by premier academic faculties.
  • Application oriented learning from SMEs: Connect pure theory with hands-on problem statements and real data models.
  • Associate with research scholars and their oceanic knowledge: Benefit from cutting-edge research methodologies from top IIT professors.
CURRICULUM BLUEPRINT

What Do You Get to Learn

Mastering foundational programming, project development, and modern machine intelligence.

01
🐍 Core Foundations

Python Workshop

A detailed workshop on fundamentals of Python along with periodic assignments. Covers syntax, data structures, algorithms, object-oriented concepts, and clean coding best practices.

02
⚙ Practical Execution

Python Mini-Project

Get hands-on experience with the intricacies and subtleties of Python. Apply your learning to engineer working utilities, data parsers, and backend workflow prototypes.

03
🤖 Intelligent Systems

Machine Learning

Learn the most sought-after skillsets in machine learning and develop your personal intelligent machine. Explore supervised & unsupervised learning, model evaluation, and predictive pipelines.
